# Expert review: make the website help people decide

September 25, 2026. Second review, starting from `711f704` after the first audit release.

The site's biggest remaining opportunity is a clearer connection between the visitor's problem, a useful demonstration, credible evidence, the right offer, and a working purchase/access journey. The black-and-gold identity is already distinctive. A wholesale visual replacement would distract from more valuable work.

This is a simulated council using published frameworks, supported by independent code reviews and observed behavior. April Dunford, Russell Brunson, Claude Hopkins and Seth Godin were not actually consulted; nothing here is an endorsement or an invented quotation. Conversion recommendations are hypotheses until measured. No revenue multiplier is promised.

## The product and proof work that should follow

Build two deliberate paths first:

1. **Practical business use:** a free, inspectable workflow → the relevant free resource → an optional next training step based on what the person wants to do.
2. **Build a useful site/app:** a small project brief → workshop details → PushTen when ongoing training/support fits. Each step should explain its own deliverables, price/billing terms and next action; provider terms must stay authoritative.

For each offer, prepare a short buyer brief: who it fits, who should skip it, the actual deliverables, what the customer must contribute, the method, access and support, limitations, price/billing terms, confirmed refund terms, and one sensible next step. Let that drive the page instead of adding sections to hit a template length.

Turn the testimonial bank into offer-specific case evidence. For example, building/technical-confidence experiences can reduce beginner hesitation, while a supported client-acquisition result belongs beside a relevant business-building offer. Preserve exact context, attribution and recorded approval; confirm which program produced the experience. Personal praise can support Brian's credibility, but should not stand in for a product result. A useful case study includes the starting problem, what the person built/did, the evidence, limitations and the relevant next action. Do not infer that a quoted individual result is typical.

The separate partner-page note remains **50% PushTen commission**, as supplied by the owner. No commission, testimonial, product record or unfinished external affiliate page was changed by this release.

## What a real commerce expansion requires

The current system supports a single next-offer chain and a separate hosted purchase. Native order bumps, one-click/off-session upsells, decline-to-downsell branches, subscriptions and randomized experiments are not secretly enabled by these design improvements. They require payment authorization/consent, idempotent state transitions, webhook recovery, refund reconciliation, access rules and verified outcome reporting. Design and test those contracts before adding promises or controls that imply they work.
